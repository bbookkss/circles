Welcome to Circles, Ben.

Here's the whole idea in three lines.

  • What a circle is

    A group that meets at the same place on a regular schedule. Volleyball every Thursday, morning pages at 7am, a run club that leaves from the same corner.

  • Finding one

    Explore shows circles near you on a map. Filter by category, day or neighbourhood. Public ones you just join.

  • Starting one

    Drop a pin and set a schedule. Public so anyone can find it, or private so you approve who joins.
