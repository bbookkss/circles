Getting started takes a minute.

  1. Find one near you.

    Every circle has an area on the map and a day it meets. Pick one close enough to walk to.

  2. Say if you're coming.

    The day before, yes or no. You see who else is in before you decide, so you're never the only one who showed.

  3. Show up.

    That's it. Come back whenever it suits you; the circle meets every week either way.
