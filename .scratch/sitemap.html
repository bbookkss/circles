Two pages nobody can currently reach

/check-email is orphaned: turning off email confirmation means signup now returns a session and goes to /welcome instead. /admin has no link anywhere in the UI, so it is URL-only by design or by accident.

/business is a dead end from the outside

The only link to it sits on your own profile page. A restaurant owner has no path to it from the landing page or explore, which is worth deciding on before flyers go out.
